Twenty‑five centuries ago, underground falaj channels at Mleiha turned scarce water into reliable date harvests and a trade hub. Today the UAE has 40 million palms and no per‑palm view of yield, revenue or water. This is the digital falaj: a live satellite scan of any farm → palms counted → tonnes and dirhams forecast → water stress mapped.
